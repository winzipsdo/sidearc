    三件要紧的事Three things that matter

    竖着排只是开始。这三件，是它的脾气。Stacking tabs vertically is only the start. These three are its character.

    其一01

    不是第二份列表，是同一份Not a second list. The same one

    Chrome 自己的标签栏跟着侧边栏走：在哪边拖，另一边都跟着动。你自己建的分组原样保留。Chrome's tab strip follows the sidebar: drag on either side and the other moves with it. Groups you made yourself are left alone.

    其二02

    工作、学习、生活，各有一套Work, study, life: a set for each

    每个空间有自己的标签页和主题色，在顶部就是一个分组。点折叠的分组或底部圆点，整个侧边栏就切过去。Every space has its own tabs and color, and shows up top as a tab group. Open its group or click its dot, and the whole sidebar switches.

    其三03

    该关的，自己会冒出来What to close shows itself

    久不去看的标签页，自会落上几片叶子：离开满一天落第一片，越久越多。随手一扫就干净，点进去就吹走。Tabs you haven't looked at gather leaves: the first falls after a full day away, and more follow. Sweep them off, or open the tab and they blow away.

    更新Updating
    在仓库里 git pull，把侧边栏关掉再打开，就是最新版本。少数更新改到了后台，到 chrome://extensions 点一下「赭」的刷新按钮；拿不准时点一下也没坏处，空间、收藏和设置都还在。Run git pull in the repository, then close and reopen the sidebar. The few updates that touch the background also need the reload button on Sweeping Monk's card in chrome://extensions; if unsure, reloading is harmless and keeps your spaces, favorites and settings.
    按自己的习惯改Make it yours
    代码是原生 JS/CSS，不用编译。哪里用着不顺手，直接让 AI 编程助手在仓库里改，比如「行再紧凑一点」；改完关掉再打开侧边栏就能看到。The code is plain JS/CSS with nothing to compile. If something doesn't fit how you work, ask an AI coding assistant to change it in the repository, say "make the rows more compact", then close and reopen the sidebar.
    放到左边On the left
    想让侧边栏在左边，打开 chrome://settings/appearance，把「侧边栏位置」改为「显示在左侧」。这是 Chrome 自己的设置，扩展没法代为修改。Want the sidebar on the left? Open chrome://settings/appearance and set the side panel position to left. It's Chrome's own setting, so the extension can't change it for you.

    快捷键Shortcuts

    不收集，不上传，没有统计，没有第三方脚本。一切都留在你的浏览器里，卸载即删除。Nothing collected, nothing uploaded, no analytics, no third-party scripts. Everything stays in your browser and goes when you uninstall.

    每项权限用来做什么，都写在这里。What each permission is for, all written down here. 完整的隐私政策The full privacy policy

    只有一处会联网，而且要你自己点：在设置里把字体换成「今楷」时，它从这个网站下载一次字体文件，存在本机，之后不再联网。It goes online in exactly one case, and only when you ask: switch the font to JinKai in settings and it downloads the font file once from this site, keeps it on your device, and fetches nothing after that.

    tabs
    读取标签页的标题、网址和图标来列出它们，并执行切换、移动、关闭、新建。Listing tabs (title, URL, icon) and switching, moving, closing and opening them.
    storage
    在本地保存空间、收藏夹、固定标签、别名，以及重启后恢复用的标签页状态。Saving spaces, favorites, pinned tabs, aliases and tab state for restoring after a restart.
    favicon

    clipboardWrite
    「复制链接地址」时把网址写入剪贴板，不读取剪贴板。"Copy link address". The clipboard is never read.
    sessions
    撤销「关闭标签页」时，从最近关闭的记录里恢复它。Restoring closed tabs on undo.
    history
    搜索时列出最近 30 天里匹配的网页；「不记录历史」的空间里，删除刚访问的网址。只在本地读，不上传。Listing matching pages from the last 30 days when you search, and removing pages just visited in no-history spaces. Read locally, never sent anywhere.
    webNavigation
    判断新标签页是不是从某个页面里的链接打开的，以及它的地址是否已经开着。Telling whether a new tab was opened from a link on a page, and whether its address is already open.
    tabGroups
    把每个空间显示成顶部标签栏里的一个分组，切换空间时折叠其他空间的分组。Showing each space as a tab group in Chrome's tab strip and collapsing the other spaces.

    名字The name

    标签页开多了，心也跟着乱。「赭」让它们各安其位；久不去看的，自会落上几片叶子，随手一扫，地净，心也净。Too many tabs, and the mind gets cluttered with them. Sweeping Monk keeps each in its place; the ones you stop visiting gather a few leaves, and one sweep clears the ground, and the mind with it.

    赭，读 zhě，是红褐色，图标那枚印的颜色；印上刻的是一个人在扫落叶，英文名 Sweeping Monk 说的也是他。它曾叫 Sidearc 和扫地僧，2026 年 11 月改成现在的名字；空间、收藏和设置都原样带了过来。The icon is a seal: a figure sweeping fallen leaves. Its Chinese name, 赭 (zhě), is the ochre of that seal. It used to be called Sidearc and took its present name in November 2026; spaces, favorites and settings all carried over.

    问答FAQ

    「赭」是什么？What is Sweeping Monk?
    一个免费开源的 Chrome 扩展：把标签页竖着排进浏览器自带的侧边栏，按空间分开，和顶部的标签栏双向同步。A free, open-source Chrome extension: it stacks your tabs in the browser's own side panel, splits them into spaces, and keeps them in two-way sync with the tab strip.
    会动我顶部的标签栏吗？Will it touch my tab strip?
    会，而且是有意的：顶部的顺序始终和侧边栏一致，收藏在最左边，每个空间一个分组。你自己建的分组原样保留。Yes, on purpose: the strip always matches the sidebar, with favorites at the far left and one tab group per space. Groups you made yourself are left as they are.
    为什么不在应用商店？Why isn't it on the Web Store?
    还在打磨，暂时只从源码或 Releases 的 zip 安装。从源码装的，更新只要一句 git pull。It's still being polished, so for now it installs from source or the zip in Releases. From source, updating is a single git pull.
    能改成自己的样子吗？Can I change it to suit me?
    能。代码是原生 JS/CSS，不用编译，以 MIT 许可证发布，改完重新打开侧边栏就生效。Yes. The code is plain JS/CSS with nothing to compile, released under the MIT License. Change it, reopen the sidebar, and it's live.
