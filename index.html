把标签页放到侧边栏Your tabs, in the sidebar

Arc 风格的 Chrome 垂直标签栏:子标签、空间、固定标签和收藏夹,和 Chrome 自己的顶部标签栏保持同步。不联网,数据只在本机。 An Arc-style vertical tabs sidebar for Chrome: child tabs, spaces, pinned tabs and favorites, kept in sync with Chrome's own tab strip. No network requests, and your data stays on your machine.

垂直标签栏Vertical tabs

标题完整可见,常用的一点即达Full titles, favorites one click away

标签页竖着排:点击切换、中键关闭、拖拽排序。顶部的收藏夹所有空间共用;固定标签关了也留着入口,再点就重新打开,还能分文件夹。 Tabs stack vertically: click to switch, middle-click to close, drag to reorder. Favorites at the top are shared by every space. Pinned tabs keep their spot when closed, reopen with a click, and can live in folders.

  • 搜索栏:输入即过滤,回车打开网址或用默认搜索引擎搜索Search box: type to filter, press Enter to open a URL or search
  • 相同网址只开一份,已经开着的直接切过去One tab per address: an open address just switches to its tab
  • 正在发声的标签页,点喇叭就能静音Tabs playing sound show a speaker you can click to mute

子标签与别名Child tabs and aliases

从哪打开,就挂在哪下面Links land under the page they came from

在网页里点开的链接,自动缩进到原标签页下面;把标签页拖到另一个标签页上也能手动归组,最多两级。给重要的标签页起个别名,显示在标题前,搜索也能搜到。 Links you open from a page are indented under it. Drag a tab onto another to nest it by hand, up to two levels. Give important tabs an alias: it shows before the title and matches in search.

  • 关闭父标签页,子标签一起关闭Closing a parent closes its children
  • 关错了?按 Ctrl/⌘+Z 一步步撤销Closed by mistake? Undo step by step with Ctrl/⌘+Z

空间与同步Spaces and tab strip sync

用空间分开工作与生活Spaces keep work and life apart

每个空间有自己的主题色、固定标签和标签页,底部圆点一键切换。Chrome 顶部的标签栏始终和侧边栏顺序一致:每个空间是一个标签页分组,当前空间展开,其他空间折叠。 Each space has its own color, pinned tabs and tabs; switch with the dots at the bottom. Chrome's tab strip always matches the sidebar: each space is a tab group, the current one expanded and the others collapsed.

  • 在哪边拖动,另一边都跟着变Drag on either side and the other one follows
  • 浅色、深色模式,跟随系统Light and dark mode, following the system
  • 浏览器重启后,空间、子标签、别名自动恢复Spaces, child tabs and aliases come back after a restart

还有这些细节And the small things

撤销Undo

关闭标签页、清理、取消固定都能撤销,Ctrl/⌘+Z 可以连续撤销多步;在顶部标签栏关掉的也能恢复。Closing tabs, Clear, unpinning and more can be undone step by step with Ctrl/⌘+Z, even tabs closed from the tab strip.

最近关闭Recently closed

左下角的时钟按钮列出最近关闭的 10 个标签页,点一下就恢复,前进后退记录也还在。The clock at the bottom left lists the last 10 closed tabs. Click one to restore it, back/forward history included.

释放内存Free up memory

右键把标签页从内存里卸掉,标签还在,点回去重新加载;文件夹和空间可以一次释放。Unload a tab from memory without closing it; it reloads when you return. Folders and spaces can unload all at once.

Keep Alive

不让 Chrome 的「节省内存」回收音乐、聊天这类要在后台一直运行的页面。Stop Chrome's Memory Saver from unloading tabs that should keep running, like music or chat.

不记录历史No-history spaces

在这样的空间或文件夹里访问的网址,会立刻从浏览历史中删除。它不是无痕模式,登录状态照常保留。Pages visited from these spaces or folders are removed from history right away. Not incognito: logins and cookies are kept.

批量关闭Close in bulk

「清理」一键关闭当前空间里除当前页以外的未固定标签页;也可以右键关闭下方所有标签页。"Clear" closes every unpinned tab in the space except the active one; or right-click to close all tabs below.

三步安装Install in three steps

需要 Chrome 116 或更高版本。界面目前是简体中文。Requires Chrome 116 or later. The interface is currently in Simplified Chinese.

  1. 打开扩展程序页面Open the extensions page

    在地址栏打开下面的地址,开启右上角的「开发者模式」。Open this address and turn on Developer mode (top right).

    chrome://extensions

  2. 加载这个文件夹Load the folder

    下载 ZIP 并解压,或者用 git 克隆;点「加载已解压的扩展程序」,选择这个文件夹。Download the ZIP and unzip it, or clone it with git; click "Load unpacked" and choose the folder.

  3. 打开侧边栏Open the sidebar

    点工具栏上的 Sidearc 图标,或者按快捷键:Click the Sidearc toolbar icon, or press:

    Alt+SWindows / Linux⌥ Option+SMac

不联网,数据只在本机No network. Your data stays local.

Sidearc 不收集、不上传任何数据,代码里没有网络请求,也没有统计或第三方脚本。空间、收藏夹、固定标签和别名都保存在浏览器本地,卸载扩展即删除。 Sidearc collects and uploads nothing: the code makes no network requests and has no analytics or third-party scripts. Spaces, favorites, pinned tabs and aliases are stored locally and removed when you uninstall.

每项权限用来做什么What each permission is for
sidePanel
全部界面都显示在浏览器侧边栏里。The whole interface lives in the side panel.
tabs
读取标签页的标题、网址和图标来列出它们,并执行切换、移动、关闭、新建。Listing tabs (title, URL, icon) and switching, moving, closing and opening them.
storage
在本地保存空间、收藏夹、固定标签、别名,以及重启后恢复用的标签页状态。Saving spaces, favorites, pinned tabs, aliases and tab state for restoring after a restart.
favicon
显示收藏夹和固定标签的网站图标。Showing site icons for favorites and pinned tabs.
search
在搜索栏输入关键词回车时,用浏览器的默认搜索引擎搜索。Searching with your default engine from the search box.
clipboardWrite
「复制链接地址」时把网址写入剪贴板,不读取剪贴板。"Copy link address". The clipboard is never read.
sessions
撤销「关闭标签页」时,从最近关闭的记录里恢复它。Restoring closed tabs on undo.
history
只用于「不记录历史」的空间和文件夹:删除刚在里面访问的网址,不读取其他历史。Only for no-history spaces and folders: deleting the pages just visited from them.
webNavigation
判断新标签页是不是从某个页面里的链接打开的(子标签),以及它的地址是否已经打开(相同地址只开一份)。Telling whether a new tab was opened from a link on a page (child tabs) and whether its address is already open (no duplicate tabs).
tabGroups
把每个空间显示成顶部标签栏里的一个标签页分组,切换空间时折叠其他空间的分组。Showing each space as a tab group in Chrome's tab strip and collapsing the other spaces.
